About Marianas Ice and Water

A family-owned business serving the Saipan community with everyday drinking water needs.

Our story

Built around family and community

Marianas Ice and Water is a family-owned business located in Papago, Saipan. We work together to provide five-gallon drinking water, convenient refills, and delivery services for our local customers.

As a family business, we understand the importance of dependable service and building trust with the people we serve. We take pride in our work and aim to make everyday water needs more convenient.

Our water process

Additional water treatment

We use a reverse osmosis system as part of our water treatment process.

The water we receive from CUC has already gone through CUC's treatment process. At Marianas Ice and Water, we further treat the water using our reverse osmosis (RO) system before providing it to our customers.
